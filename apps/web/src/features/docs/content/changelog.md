Player-facing changes, grouped by the day they shipped. Newest first.

## November 12, 2026

- The Exchange loads faster: skill cards arrive with the first page instead of after it.
- Clearer Exchange field labels, and a centred sign-in card.

## November 11, 2026

- `/verify` offers recent ranked matches to check, so you can try a verification without a match id of your own.
- Casual and practice results offer a log check instead of promising a ranked record they don't have.
- Practice against the bot accepts the match by itself.

## November 10, 2026

- The PvP prize pool explains how it fills, in plain words.
- The Play home and the look editor show each agent's own role.

## November 9, 2026

- Signed-out visitors see what each screen is for, with links to the public pages and a bot-vs-bot showreel.
- Tournaments show a sign-in card instead of a dead end when you are signed out.
- The Exchange lists skills for sale first, with "Not for sale" listings after.

## November 8, 2026

- **Agent gestures**: agents salute a live command, flourish when a skill fires, look where they are going, point at callouts, huddle at kick-off and hurry in the last ticks.
- Crews are described as multi-agentic across the site.

## November 7, 2026

- **Match-start cinematic**: a camera sweep across the map, then both crews as name cards.
- **Debrief highlight clip**: the match's best moment, looped like a trailer.
- **Intent icons** by each nameplate show what every agent is going for. Click anything on the live board to see what it is.
- **Agent reactions** to bluffs, overheard lines and hazard calls, with crew poses in the debrief.
- **Sound and music**: match effects over a background music bed, with separate toggles.
- Agents talk, listen, think, cheer, point, face off and have idle habits.
- Walking animations, a clearer third-person view and board zoom that centres on your crew.
- The queue refuses a crew you can't afford before the match starts.
- The home deck is simpler, with one first-steps bulb and a bot showreel below.
- Tables turn into cards on phones.
- Dialogs are never clipped, and Play deck tiles never overlap.

## November 6, 2026

- **Agent looks everywhere**: cosmetics show across the game, and you can rename your agents.
- **Agent reaction emotes** in the arena, third person and the feed.
- **Model choice per agent** in casual and practice, priced by the server. Ranked is unchanged.
- **Public Exchange**: signed-out visitors can browse skills.
- **Gasless onboarding** for new players, switched off until a relayer is funded.
- Payment fixes: a top-up survives a page reload, cancelled listings and retries no longer double charge.
- Fixes for stuck credit holds and debrief navigation.
- Verify is on the PvP hub, and placeholder "Soon" sections are gone.

## November 5, 2026

- **Writer backing**: back a skill writer for a capped share of their future royalties.
- **Verify in the browser**: check a match against the ranked record at `/verify`.
- **Agent rentals** paid with credits.
- **Map editor and community maps**: paint a mirrored map, test-play it and publish it.
- **Puzzle mode**: six fixed boards, solved in the fewest ticks, with medals.
- **Daily challenge**: one seed per day against the bot, with a global board.
- **Campaign**: chapters of missions against the bot that unlock starter skills.
- **Hybrid control**: steer one agent yourself while the AI runs the rest.
- **Supply tools**: sandbags, pumps and bridges from supply drops.
- **New hazards**: gas, ice and electricity through water.
- **Agent progression**: XP, levels and traits.
- **Quests, play streaks and achievements**, with soulbound badges.
- **Skill test bench**: run a skill against the bot, edit, re-run and compare.
- Sign out from the account card in the top bar.

## November 4, 2026

- **Bring your own agent** to casual and practice, through a pull API or the MCP server.
- **Any model**: skirm works with any OpenAI-compatible provider and model.
- **Crew playbooks**: one shared crew strategy per player, picked at lock-in.
- **Role skill slots**: skills declare which roles may equip them.
- **Skill triggers**: a skill can fire only when a condition holds.
- **Private lobbies and tournaments**: friend challenges by code and Elo-seeded brackets with prize pools.
- **Shareable replays**: public links, clips, highlights and a caster.
- **Draft mode**: a casual queue where each crew bans one rival skill.
- **Debrief decisions**: "why did it do that?", a per-agent decision timeline.
- **Ranked tiers**: placements, Elo decay, and map and skill leaderboards.
- **Onboarding**: a guided first match, starter skill templates and a first-steps checklist.
- **Live commands**: a few orders to your crew during a match.
- Wallet connect in one click.
- Legal pages, plus account export and deletion.

## November 3, 2026

- **Agent mind**: the planner's trace and the agent's inner voice in the mind panel.
- Practice shows every mind, and talk rates follow measured usage.
- **Agent rentals** for a single match.

## November 2, 2026

- **Bluffs** live in matches and in the agents' prompts.
- The match report shows the season points a ranked result gave.
- **Agent record**: season and all-time results, including bluffs.
- Maps, practice difficulty and bluffs tuned.

## November 1, 2026

- First build: the play deck, the Exchange, the agent editor as a character screen, and the map screens.
